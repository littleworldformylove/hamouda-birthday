August 26, 2026 · The day we met

It started with a few exchanged glances on the beach.

I was about to leave, never expecting that you would come up to me. You started a conversation, and somehow, I did something I had never done before — I offered you my number first.

Well, technically, you gave me yours.

Later that evening, we met again at the hotel restaurant. We talked for hours, about everything and nothing. Then we walked towards the beach, sat on a sunbed, listened to music, and watched the sea, the moon, and the stars.

You walked me back to my room that night.

And just like that, an ordinary day became a memory I'll always keep.

Et voilà, notre histoire a commencé.

Chapter V · From me to you

My love,

Happy birthday, baby. I wish I could be next to you today, see your smile when you wake up, hug you a little longer, and celebrate you in person.

Since I can't do that right now, I wanted to make you something you could keep — a little corner of the internet filled with pieces of a memory that means so much to me, and words I hope will make you feel loved.

Sometimes I think about how close I was to simply walking away from the beach that day without knowing you. I'm so glad our paths crossed, and that one unexpected conversation turned into a night I'll always remember.

I hope this new year of your life brings you happiness, good health, beautiful opportunities, and everything your heart is wishing for. Keep being exactly who you are. You deserve so many good things.

And whenever you look at the stars, remember that somewhere under the same sky, someone is thinking of you.

All my love,
your baby ♡
